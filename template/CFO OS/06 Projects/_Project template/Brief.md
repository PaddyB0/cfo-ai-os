# Brief

Copy the whole `_Project template` folder to begin a project, and rename the copy for the project in plain English. Fill in every heading below before the work begins.

## Purpose

What the project is for, in two or three sentences.

## Owner

Who asked for it and who signs it off.

## Due

The date it must be done by, and why that date.

## Scope

What is in.

## Out of scope

What is not, so it does not creep in.

## Inputs

What is needed, from whom, and by when. Files received go in `Inputs/` as received.

## Done when

How everyone will know it is finished. When it is, move the folder to `08 Archive`.
