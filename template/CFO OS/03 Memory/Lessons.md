# Lessons

Corrections the CFO made and mistakes not to repeat: a metric read the wrong way, a figure that did not tie, a style the reader did not like. Newest first.

## Line format

`- yyyy-mm-dd: what went wrong. What to do instead. Source: the output or session where it was corrected.`

A lesson that should hold every time may become a rule. Propose it for `02 Rules and style/Rules.md`, and write it there only on the CFO's yes.
