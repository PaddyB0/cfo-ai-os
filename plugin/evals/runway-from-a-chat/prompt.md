Fictional company, figures in GBP thousands. Closing cash at the end of March 2027 was 1,200 (actuals). Our 3+9 forecast has these net cash movements: Apr -140, May -150, Jun +60, Jul -160, Aug -170, Sep -120, Oct -180, Nov -150, Dec -90. We must keep at least 250 in the bank. The last three months of actual burn were -110, -125 and -115. How long does the cash last, and what happens if customer receipts come in 20 a month lower?
